# Research

Scope: **project**, optional, before the grill.

Some ideas cannot be interviewed yet because the answers live outside the room.
Research is where you go and get them, then come back with facts the grill can
use.

It is not a station. Most projects skip it, and skipping is the normal case.

## When it is worth doing

- The idea depends on a platform's rules or a service's price.
- There are three plausible ways to build it and nobody knows the difference.
- The owner keeps saying "I think it works like ..." about something checkable.
- A previous attempt failed and nobody knows why.

## What it produces

A page in the vault, not a chat summary. If the harness has a research or explore
mode, let it do the reading. The findings still land here, in the vault, not in
the repo. Where it goes:

- A project's own research: the project page, or a page beside it.
- A subject with no project yet: a topic vault, which is the same idea he uses
  for research databases to build on later.

The page holds what was checked, where it came from, and what it rules out. Dates
on anything that goes stale: prices, versions, policies.

## Rules

- **A source per claim**, or it is still a guess.
- **Say what it rules out.** Research that only adds options makes the interview
  longer, not shorter.
- **Time-box it.** Enough to answer the questions the grill would ask. Research
  with no stopping point is how a project starts with a week of reading and no
  spec.
- **Write it down.** A finding that lives only in the session is not research, it
  is a conversation.

## Completion criterion

The open questions from the brief have answers with sources, in the vault, and
the grill can now proceed without guessing.
